# Electrical Schematic Description

## System Power

Main supply:

```text
+5V_ANALOG
GND
```

Reference voltage:

```text
VMID = 2.5 V
```

VMID is generated by:

```text
R1 = 10 kΩ from +5V_ANALOG to VMID
R2 = 10 kΩ from VMID to GND
C1 = 10 µF from VMID to GND
C2 = 100 nF from VMID to GND
U5A = TLV9062 voltage follower
```

The output of U5A is the buffered VMID reference.

All audio signals in the single-supply circuit are centered around:

```text
VMID = 2.5 V
```

---

## Microphone Channel 1

```text
MIC1
  ↓
C3 AC coupling capacitor
  ↓
R3 bias resistor
  ↓
U1A microphone preamplifier
  ↓
MIC1_PREAMP
  ↓
Analog recording switch
  ↓
RECORD_SIGNAL_1
  ↓
Record driver 1
  ↓
Record head 1
  ↓
Tape Track 1
```

The microphone signal is biased around VMID.

Initial preamplifier gain:

```text
Gain ≈ 10
```

The gain must remain adjustable during calibration.

Suggested gain resistor arrangement:

```text
R4 = 10 kΩ
R5 = 90 kΩ
```

Approximate non-inverting gain:

\[
G=1+\frac{R5}{R4}
\]

## Recording Gate

Arduino outputs:

```text
RECORD_GATE
```

Logic:

```text
RECORD_GATE = HIGH:
    Both recording switches ON

RECORD_GATE = LOW:
    Both recording switches OFF
```

Connections:

```text
Arduino D8 → Record Gate 1 control
Arduino D8 → Record Gate 2 control
```

Use a buffer if required.

The Arduino does not drive the audio signal directly.

The signal path is:

```text
Microphone preamplifier
→ Analog switch
→ Record driver
→ Magnetic head
```

---

## Tape Tracks

The tape contains two parallel tracks:

```text
TRACK_1 = MIC1
TRACK_2 = MIC2
```

Both tracks are recorded during the same Gate interval.

Frame format:

```text
[Guard Gap]
[Mic 1 + Mic 2 Audio Frame]
[Guard Gap]
```

The two audio frames must have the same start and end time.

## Difference Amplifier

Inputs:

```text
READ_ALIGNED_1
READ_ALIGNED_2
```

Output:

```text
VERROR = READ_ALIGNED_1 - READ_ALIGNED_2
```

Use U4A as a matched-resistor difference amplifier.

Initial gain:

```text
G = 1
```

All four difference-amplifier resistors should be matched.

Recommended initial resistor network:

```text
R7 = R8 = R9 = R10 = 10 kΩ
```

Use 0.1% resistors or a matched resistor network.

---

## Analog Squarer

```text
VERROR
  ↓
ADL5391
  ↓
VSQUARE
```

The desired relationship is:

\[
V_\text{SQUARE}
\propto
V_\text{ERROR}^{2}
\]

The squarer input must remain within the allowed input range of the selected multiplier.

Add an input attenuator if required.

---

## Energy Integrator

```text
VSQUARE
  ↓
R11 input resistor
  ↓
U4B integrator
  ↓
ENERGY_VALUE
```

Use:

```text
R11 = initial value to be selected
C7 = initial value to be selected
R12 = discharge resistor parallel with C7
```

The integration time constant is approximately:

\[
\tau=R12 \times C7
\]

The integration window is controlled by the frame timing.

---

## Sample and Hold

At the end of the valid frame:

```text
Arduino SAMPLE_PULSE
        ↓
Analog switch
        ↓
Hold capacitor
        ↓
U5B buffer
        ↓
FRAME_ENERGY_OUTPUT
```

The energy value is held until the next frame.

Arduino output:

```text
D9 → SAMPLE_PULSE
```

---

## Erase Control

After the frame has been read and processed:

```text
Arduino ERASE_GATE
        ↓
Erase driver
        ↓
Erase head 1
Erase head 2
```

Required order:

```text
Record
→ Buffer
→ Playback
→ Energy integration
→ Sample and Hold
→ Erase
```

The erase head must not reach the frame before the energy value is stored.

---

## Arduino Signals

```text
D8  = RECORD_GATE
D9  = SAMPLE_PULSE
D10 = ERASE_GATE
D11 = SYNC_CONTROL
```

Suggested logic:

```text
RECORD_GATE:
    HIGH during audio recording

SAMPLE_PULSE:
    Short pulse after playback and integration

ERASE_GATE:
    HIGH only after processing is complete

SYNC_CONTROL:
    Frame marker or timing reference
```

---

## Grounding

Use a star-ground arrangement:

```text
Analog ground
Playback ground
Record-driver ground
Arduino ground
Motor ground
```

The motor ground must not share long high-current paths with the playback preamplifier ground.

## Important Unfinished Circuits

The following circuits must be designed after measuring the selected tape heads:

```text
Record-head driver
Record-bias oscillator
Erase-head driver
Motor controller
Tape-speed sensor
Head mechanical mounting
```

Do not connect a magnetic record head directly to an Arduino pin or TLV9062 output.

## First Electrical Test

Before using magnetic tape:

```text
1. Replace Mic 1 with a signal generator.
2. Replace Mic 2 with a delayed or phase-shifted signal.
3. Test both recording gates.
4. Test the subtractor.
5. Test the squarer.
6. Test the integrator.
7. Verify FRAME_ENERGY_OUTPUT.
```

Only after this test passes should the tape heads be connected.
